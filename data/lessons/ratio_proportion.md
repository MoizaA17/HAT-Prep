# Ratio & Proportion

**Reading time:** about 5 minutes
**HAT pace:** about 72 seconds per question

---

## 1. What you must know first

**Ratio.** A ratio compares two or more quantities of the same kind. "Boys to girls is 2:3" means that for every 2 boys there are 3 girls. Think of the numbers as **parts**.

**Simplify a ratio** by dividing all its numbers by the same number: 20:30 = 2:3.

**One part is the key.** Every ratio question becomes easy once you find the value of **one part**.

- If a total is shared in a ratio: one part = total / (sum of the ratio numbers)
- If one share is known: one part = known share / its ratio number

**Proportion** says two ratios are equal. There are two kinds:

| Type | What happens | What stays the same |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** | One quantity goes up, the other goes up too (more pens, more cost) | The ratio y / x |
| **Inverse** | One quantity goes up, the other goes down (more speed, less time) | The product x x y |

**Ratio of changes.** If a ratio changes after adding or removing something, do not add to the ratio numbers. Write the quantities as multiples (like 2x and 5x), add the change to the real quantities, then solve.

---

## 2. Worked example 1: sharing in three parts

**Question:** Rs. 840 is shared among Usman, Fatima and Omar in the ratio 2:3:7. How much does Omar get?

**Steps:**

1. Add the parts: 2 + 3 + 7 = 12 parts
2. Value of one part = 840 / 12 = Rs. 70
3. Omar has 7 parts = 7 x 70 = **Rs. 490**
4. Quick check: Usman 140 + Fatima 210 + Omar 490 = 840 (matches the total)

**Answer: Rs. 490**

---

## 3. Worked example 2: inverse proportion

**Question:** A car going at 60 km/h takes 4.5 hours for a journey. How long will it take at 90 km/h?

**Steps:**

1. More speed means less time, so this is **inverse** proportion
2. Find the fixed quantity, the distance: 60 x 4.5 = 270 km
3. New time = distance / new speed = 270 / 90 = **3 hours**
4. Quick check: speed went up by a factor of 1.5 (60 to 90), so time must go down by the same factor: 4.5 / 1.5 = 3

**Answer: 3 hours**

---

## 4. Short trick: the "constant check"

**The trick:** Before you calculate, ask one question: **what stays constant?**

- **Direct proportion:** the ratio (y / x) stays constant. Cross-multiply: if 4 items cost 100, then 10 items cost 10 x 100 / 4 = 250.
- **Inverse proportion:** the product (x x y) stays constant. Multiply the first pair, then divide by the new number.

This also lets you throw out wrong options fast. If more workers, more speed or more taps should mean *less* time, any option showing *more* time is wrong.

### When it WORKS

Use it only when the question says or clearly means **"at the same rate"**:

- Price per item is fixed (no extra charges)
- Speed is steady
- Every worker or machine works equally fast
- The scale on a map or a recipe is fixed

### When it does NOT work

Do **not** use it when:

1. **There is a fixed charge plus a rate.** A taxi that charges Rs. 50 plus Rs. 20 per km is not proportional. A 3 km ride costs 110, but a 6 km ride costs 170, not 220.
2. **The rate changes.** Examples: speed changes during the journey, discounts for bulk buying, or tax slabs.
3. **Workers are not equally efficient.** For example, a man and a boy working together, or a machine that slows down.
4. **A ratio changes after adding or removing something** (ages after some years, adding water to a mixture). Here the ratio itself is not constant. Use multiples (2x, 5x) and add to the real quantities.
5. **You see "increase by" or "decrease by".** First check whether the question means a change in the ratio or a percentage change.

**Warning sign:** if the question mentions a "fixed", "base", "joining" or "minimum" amount, stop and check.

## 5. Practice questions

Try each one in under 72 seconds. Cover the answers first.

**Practice 1**
The ratio of two numbers is 5:7 and their difference is 18. What is the larger number?

- A. 45
- B. 54
- C. 63
- D. 90

**Practice 2**
A taxi charges a fixed Rs. 50 plus Rs. 20 for every km. A 3 km ride costs Rs. 110. What is the cost of a 7 km ride?

- A. Rs. 140
- B. Rs. 190
- C. Rs. 257
- D. Rs. 256.67

---

### Answers

**Practice 1: C (63)**

1. The difference in parts = 7 - 5 = 2 parts
2. 2 parts = 18, so 1 part = 9
3. Larger number = 7 x 9 = 63 (the smaller is 5 x 9 = 45, and 63 - 45 = 18)

**Practice 2: B (Rs. 190)**

1. This has a fixed charge, so the constant check does **not** work. Cost per km is not the same as total cost / km.
2. Cost = 50 + (20 x 7) = 50 + 140 = Rs. 190
3. The trap: 110 x 7 / 3 = 256.67 is the proportion mistake (option D). Option C is the same wrong answer rounded up.
